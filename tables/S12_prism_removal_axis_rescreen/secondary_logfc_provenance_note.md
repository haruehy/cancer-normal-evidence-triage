# Secondary logFC provenance and regeneration scope

The Secondary logFC removal-axis results are regenerated by `analysis/07_prism_removal_axis_rescreen.py` when the raw Secondary logFC matrix is available under `data/secondary-screen-replicate-collapsed-logfold-change.csv`, `data/secondary-screen-logfold-change.csv`, or the corresponding `data/raw/PRISM/` path recorded in Repository Table S14.

The lightweight repository bundle does not redistribute the raw PRISM Secondary logFC matrix. In lightweight validation mode, bundled CSV outputs are checked for presence and manuscript consistency; full from-raw regeneration requires the separately downloaded raw PRISM file.
